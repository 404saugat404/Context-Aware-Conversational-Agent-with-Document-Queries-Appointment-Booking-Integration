Company Overview

TechCare Solutions is a healthcare technology company founded in 2020. We specialize in building digital health platforms that connect patients with healthcare providers. Our headquarters is located in Kathmandu, Nepal.

Services We Offer

We provide the following services:
1. Telemedicine Consultations - Virtual appointments with licensed doctors via video call.
2. Health Record Management - Secure digital storage and management of patient medical records.
3. Appointment Scheduling - Online booking system for in-person and virtual visits.
4. Prescription Management - Digital prescription creation, tracking, and pharmacy integration.
5. Lab Report Integration - Automated lab result delivery and interpretation.
